LIGHT / COLOR / CHARACTER

01 / EMULSION

Find your film.

From the subtle color of Portra to the saturation of Velvia, the glow of 800T and the texture of HP5. Search twenty interpretations of Kodak, Fujifilm, CineStill, Ilford and Lomography films.

02 / CHARACTER

Follow your eye.

Shape color balance, split toning and faded blacks. Push grain to 800%, shape bloom and halation. Place light leaks directly on your photograph. Match a reference photograph’s color and tone, save your look as a recipe, and batch develop a whole session.

03 / YOUR DARKROOM

Keep it yours.

Work locally, without an account. Compare with movable splits or side-by-side views, zoom up to 400%, undo adjustments and export full-resolution JPEG, PNG or 16-bit TIFF. Your original files remain untouched.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

From download
to first frame.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Romeling Silverroom into Applications.
  2. Launch the app, then import a photograph with ⌘O or drag it into the window.
  3. Choose a film. Adjust the feeling. Export when it looks right.
